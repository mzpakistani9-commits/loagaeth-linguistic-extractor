# Hildegard of Bingen — Lingua Ignota (c. 1150 CE)

## Overview
- First constructed language in Western history. ~900 glossed nouns.
- Riesencodex (Wiesbaden HS 2, c. 1190–1200) is the main manuscript.
- 23 "Litterae Ignotae" letters in 1:1 mapping with Latin letters.
- Grammar is pure Latin; the vocabulary is invented.

## Key Vocabulary
- Aizniz (God), Aieganz (angel), Crizanz (Christ), Maiz (man), Vilbez (woman),
  Galderich (jaundice), Loibenz (lion), Zilzibriz (wax), Bursiol (eye),
  Orechons (mouth), Linschiol (Latin language).
- Scholar reference: Sarah Higley, *Hildegard of Bingen's Unknown Language* (Palgrave Macmillan, 2007).

## Working Guidance
- When analysing Lingua Ignota, distinguish the 23 invented letters from Latin grammar.
- Russian/Eastern books frequently mislabeled it angelic; mark provenance uncertainty as [UNDECIPHERED].
