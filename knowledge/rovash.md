# Hungarian Rovás (Székely-Hungarian Rovás)

## Overview
- RTL carved script in use 4th–18th century CE (Old Hungarian), carved into wood/stone.
- 12 distinct regional variants documented by Fischer Károly Antal (1889).

## CRITICAL READING KEY
- **Hungarian s = English SH**
- **Hungarian sz = English S**
- Failing this distinction produces systematic misreadings of every inscription.

## Digraphs & Special Letters
- cs (ch), gy (palatal dy), ly (palatal l), ny (ñ), ty (palatal t), zs (zh).
- Vowels include separate glyphs for short/long forms in some variants.

## Key Inscriptions
- Nickelsburg inscription (c. 1490)
- Bologna Rovás (1515)
- Énlaka Unitarian inscription (1668)

## Working Guidance
- First determine the variant (of Fischer's 12) before mapping glyphs.
- Translate s/sz correctly; vowel glyph presence varies by variant.
- Mark ambiguous glyph-to-sound assignments as POSSIBLE, not CONFIRMED.
