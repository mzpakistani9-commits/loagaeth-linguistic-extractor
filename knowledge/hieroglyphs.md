# Egyptian Hieroglyphs — Gardiner & the Fabricius Workbench

## Gardiner Sign Categories
A=man, B=woman, C=deity, D=body, E=mammals, F=mammal parts, G=birds, H=bird parts,
I=reptiles, K=fish, M=plants, N=sky/earth/water, O=buildings, P=ships,
Q=furniture, S=crowns/dress, T=warfare, U=agriculture, V=rope/fiber, W=vessels,
X=bread, Y=writing, Z=strokes, Aa=unclassified. 750+ signs total.

## Phonetics
- Uniliterals (one consonant): e.g. G17 (Owl = m), N35 (water ripple = n), X1 (bread loaf = t), O1 (house plan = pr).
- Biliterals and triliterals combine consonant sequences.
- Berlin-Brandenburg Academy dictionary (CC BY-SA 4.0) is the authoritative modern source.

## Google Fabricius Workbench
- Open-source Angular tool (googleartsculture/workbench). Live: fabriciusworkbench.withgoogle.com.
- Workflow: upload → facsimile → segment → classify (Gardiner) → translate.
- Partners: Macquarie University, Ubisoft.

## Scripts of Egypt
- Hieroglyphic (monumental), Hieratic (cursive), Demotic (late shorthand), Coptic (final stage, Greek-derived).
- Rosetta Stone = trilingual key (Hieroglyphic, Demotic, Greek).

## Working Guidance
- Provide sign-by-sign: category, form, phonetic value, Berlin-Brandenburg translation.
- Separate phonetic (Gardiner) reading from determinative function.
- Mark uncertain sign identifications as POSSIBLE; unknown readings [UNDECIPHERED].
- Cross-religious parallels only where transliteration justifies them.
