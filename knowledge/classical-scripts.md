# Classical & Deciphered Ancient Scripts — Quick Reference

## Mesopotamian
- Sumerian Cuneiform: earliest writing (c. 3200 BCE, Uruk). Logographic + syllabic. Key text: Gilgamesh, administrative tablets.
- Akkadian/Babylonian: Semitic, syllabic cuneiform. Behistun/trilingual Rosetta-style aids.
- Hittite Cuneiform: Indo-European, borrowed cuneiform.

## Mediterranean & Near East
- Linear B (Mycenaean Greek): deciphered 1952 by Ventris (consonant×vowel grid technique). Syllabic ~90 signs + ideograms.
- Phoenician: 22-letter consonantal alphabet, ancestor of Greek/Latin. Direction RTL.
- Ugaritic: 30-letter cuneiform alphabet (RTL), c. 1300 BCE.
- Aramaic: imperial lingua franca, ancestor of Nabataean→Arabic and Syriac script.
- Etruscan: readable letters (from Greek), language isolate; Luwian: Anatolian hieroglyphs (deciphered).

## Persia & the Americas
- Old Persian Cuneiform: semi-alphabetic, deciphered via Behistun.
- Mayan Glyphs: logosyllabic, deciphered (Knorozov); thousands of glyphs; calendar + dynastic records.

## East Asia
- Oracle Bone Script: earliest Chinese writing, Shang dynasty (c. 1200 BCE).
- Classical Chinese: literary language until 1911.
- Sanskrit/Brahmi/Pali: Brahmi is ancestor of most Indic scripts; Kharosthi the Gandharan script.

## Europe
- Elder Futhark (24 runes, c. 150 CE), Younger Futhark (16 runes, Viking age).
- Ogham: early Ireland, 20 letters in strokes along an edge.
- Glagolitic: Old Church Slavonic alphabet (Cyril 863 CE); Gothic: Wulfila's 4th c. alphabet from Greek.
