# Angel Runic — Archaic Spiriform Script

## Construction Principle
- Every letter is built from circles and/or spirals ("spiriform").
- 26 base letters plus digraphs: bb, ch, ff, gg, ph, rr, sh, th, tt.

## Divine Symbols
- God (6-pointed star), Devil (inverted pentagram), Fire (triple flame),
  Wind (3 waves), Earth (cross-in-square), Water (3 wavy lines),
  Love (heart), Pray (hands), Christ (cross), Trinity (Ψ/trident),
  Wing/Angel (Ψ), Brother of Adam (△), Sister of Eve (▽), Holy Legion (⚔).

## Context & Caveats
- A modern esoteric system (as published for spirit communication), not an attested ancient script.
- Label provenance honestly: mark it "constructed modern esoteric" rather than presenting as historical epigraphy.
- When a symbol is ambiguous, provide the spiriform construction analysis and mark [UNDECIPHERED].
