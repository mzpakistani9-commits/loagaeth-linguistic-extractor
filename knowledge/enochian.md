# Enochian Language — Dee & Kelley (1583–84)

## Alphabet (21 Letters)
21 letters: Un(A), Pe(B), Veh(C/K), Gon(D), Or(E), Med(F), Mals(G), Tal(H), Gal(I/Y), Urs(K), Don(R), Fam(S), Vran(N), Graph(O), Tal2(P), Ged(G/J), Gisg(T), Mals2(U/V/W), Med2(X/Z). No J, U, W as distinct graphemes. Letter names form meaningful words (Un = "understanding", Gon = "strength"). Each letter maps to one of the four classical elements cycling Fire→Water→Air→Earth.

## Grammar & Morphosyntax
- Word order: VSO (verb-subject-object) — rare typologically, parallels Classical Arabic, Biblical Hebrew, Irish.
- Confirmed grammatical markers: -O = genitive ("IAD-O" = of God), -AX = participial (ZACAR-AX), -ES = vocative (GAH-ES), OD = connector "and" (847× in corpus), NA = negation, NAX = emphatic negation.
- Index of Coincidence (IC) = 0.082.

## Tier-1 Confirmed Lexicon (264 words)
IAD (God), OL (I/command), SONF (reign), ZACAR (move/open), ZAMRAN (show thyself), NIIS (come), GANS (messenger), OR (light), OD (and), NA (no), NAX (negation), ALLA (totality), GRAA (moon), ADON (Lord), DOXA (glory), IAN (I AM), TARDEMAH (deep sleep), BALT (justice), VOOAN (truth), VONPH (wrath).
Reversal pairs (lexical antithesis): DAM↔MAD, LOT↔TOL — unique among attested languages.

## Four Watchtowers (Elemental Tablets)
Each Watchtower is a 12×13 grid: Air = BATAIVAH / ORO IBAH AOZPI; Fire = EDLPRNAA / OIP TEAA PDOCE; Water = RAAGIOSL / MPH ARSL GAIOL; Earth = ICZHIHAL / MOR DIAL HCTGA. Each contains 3 holy names, a King name, and 6 Seniors.

## 30 Aethyrs & 91 Governors
Aethyrs: LIL, ARN, ZOM, PAZ, LIT, MAZ, DEO, ZID, ZIP, ZAX, then 20 more up to TEX (nearest Earth). Each Aethyr contains Governors extracted from the Great Table by taking 4–7 letters and adding endings -L, -R, -V, -Y.

## Sigillum Dei Aemeth
7 heptagonal rings, pentagram, 40 outer angels. Sources: Reeds 1996, Harkness 1999, Peterson 2003, Laycock 1994, Casaubon 1659.

## Related Angelic Scripts
Celestial Alphabet (Agrippa), Malachim, Passing the River, and Theban Alphabet are grimoire alphabets historically shared with Enochian/angelic tradition. When analysing these, apply the same angelic-letter framework and the honesty rule ([UNDECIPHERED] where origins are uncertain).
